# Offline earnings

Your businesses keep working after you close the game. You don't need to stay online.

## The 24-hour rule

Businesses run for up to 24 hours after you were last online. Then they pause until you come back.

Open the game once a day and nothing ever pauses.

## What keeps running

During those 24 hours everything works as if you were there:

- Carts, shops and restaurants sell to customers.
- Farms, mines and factories produce.
- Rent comes in from tenants.
- Wages, rent, running costs and property tax are paid.
- Shipments arrive and exports are paid.
- Your open orders on the exchange can still fill.

## What pauses after 24 hours

A paused business does nothing. It makes no sales and no goods.

It also costs nothing. No wages, rent or running costs are charged while it is paused.

Rent from your buildings and property tax pause too.

You are not punished for a break. Everything starts again the moment you return.

## Getting the most from time away

Customers can only buy what is in stock. Before you log off:

1. Fill your storage with enough ingredients for a full day.
2. Keep enough cash for a day of wages, rent and running costs.
3. Make sure mines and factories have diesel and inputs.
4. Check that producers have storage space, or turn on auto-sell.
5. Set opening hours to match busy times, so wages aren't wasted.

## What can go wrong while you are away

- An ingredient runs out and a dish stops selling.
- Cash runs out. Shop staff then quit, and other businesses close until you reopen them.
- Rent stays unpaid. After 3 days of unpaid rent a lease ends.
- Storage fills up and extra output is lost.

You get a notification for each of these. Read them when you come back.

## If you delete your account

Your businesses stay on the map as abandoned buildings. They no longer trade.
