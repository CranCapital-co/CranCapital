# Food carts

A food cart is your first business. It costs $5,000 and parks on any street or path.

## Parking your cart

- Walk to the spot first. You must stand within 25 metres of it.
- Carts park on streets and paths, not inside buildings or on water.
- Two carts can't park closer than 12 metres to each other.
- Busy spots sell more. Look near stations, sights and shops.
- You can move a cart later if the spot turns out to be quiet.

## The menu

A cart sells street food. A level 1 cart has 3 menu slots. Each sale uses ingredients from your storage in that city.

| Dish | Ingredients per sale | Fair price | Ingredient cost at base prices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Garden Salad | 3 lettuce, 2 tomato | $12.00 | $0.48 |
| Veggie Wrap | 1 flour, 2 lettuce, 1 tomato | $15.00 | $0.48 |
| Hot Chips | 3 potato | $10.50 | $0.21 |
| Banana Smoothie | 3 banana | $13.50 | $0.45 |
| Pumpkin Soup | 2 pumpkin | $13.50 | $0.66 |
| Onigiri | 2 rice, 1 wasabi | $9.00 | $0.24 |
| Coffee to Go | 1 coffee beans | $9.00 | $0.40 |

Market traders sell about 10% above the base price, so expect to pay a little more.

## Opening hours and busy times

A new cart opens from 7:00 to 23:00, in the city's real local time. You can change the hours.

Carts are busiest at lunch, from about 12:00 to 14:00. There is a smaller evening rush around 18:00. Weekends bring about 15% more customers.

## Running costs

A cart costs $2 an hour to run. Carts need no staff and pay no rent.

If you can't cover the running costs, the cart closes. Add money and reopen it.

## Upgrades

| Level | Menu slots | Customers served per hour | Upgrade cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | up to 120 | (bought for $5,000) |
| 2 | 4 | up to 200 | $7,500 |
| 3 | 6 | up to 300 | $15,000 |

Each level also makes the cart a little more attractive to customers. Running costs rise by half the base rate per level.

## Limits and selling

- You can run at most 5 carts.
- You can sell a cart back to the city for 50% of what you spent on it.
