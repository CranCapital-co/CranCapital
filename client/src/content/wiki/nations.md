# Nations: lead a country

Every player leads a real country, in one shared world. Countries without a player are run by AI governments.

**Choosing a country.** Countries are first come, first served, and each has one leader. You can claim any country an AI runs, even one an AI has conquered. You can't claim a country that another player holds.

Claiming a country gives you a fresh start:

- your homeland;
- a home army and barracks;
- 24 hours of protection while you settle in. Declaring war yourself ends it.

If you step down, you can lead again after 12 hours. If you are away for 14 days, the AI takes over.

**Your economy.** Your country and your businesses share one purse: your own cash. Every hour your land's taxes are paid to you, after the share you set for research and for infrastructure (which slowly raises income). Army and building upkeep come out of your cash. If you can't pay it, some troops desert. Everything military you buy is paid from your cash too. Your money-per-second counter includes your country's earnings.

**Stability (0 to 100).** Stability raises income and recruits.

- High taxes, wars, war exhaustion and lost land lower it.
- Peace, alliances, low taxes and Propaganda raise it.
- Below 35, land you occupy without troops can rise up.

**Military.** Build in your own territory.

- **Units:** barracks train infantry. An arms factory builds artillery and armour, an airbase trains air wings, a naval base (coast only) builds fleets, and a silo builds missiles.
- **Defences:** forts add 15% defence a level. Minefields (from level 4) destroy 6% of attacking infantry, artillery and armour a level before the battle, up to level 3. Air defence weakens air attacks and stops missiles.
- **Labs:** they speed up research.
- **Manpower:** recruits cost money and manpower, which grows with your population. Small countries rely on money and technology rather than numbers.

**Research.** 21 technologies across land, air, sea, defence, strategy and economy. Each one has a price, paid once from your cash when you start it (switching away and back is free). Research builds up even when nothing is selected.

**War.**

- **Declaring war:** needs Renown level 5. The target's allies join them. You can't attack a country you have a truce or pact with, or a new leader still under protection.
- **Moving troops:** troops cross one border (land or sea) at a time. On the world map, badges show how many troops you (gold), your allies (green) and your enemies (red) have in each territory, and each army on the move shows its size and how far it has got. The Military tab lists your armies with a progress bar, and each war shows how far your invasion has reached. Air wings reach 2,500 km and missiles 3,000 km.
- **Battles:** your attack is compared with their defence (troops, local militia and forts). The stronger side wins and loses less. Attacking across the sea without a fleet costs 40% of your attack. A ground victory occupies the territory.
- **Odds:** the Send troops window shows them before you attack.
- **War score:** grows with the land you occupy and the battles you win.
- **Peace:** propose a white peace (everyone goes home), demand the land you hold, or demand reparations. You can also surrender, which gives the enemy what they hold and a quarter of your cash. A country that loses all its land falls. Peace brings a 24-hour truce.

**Diplomacy.**

- **Envoys:** each costs $2,500 and improves relations.
- **Denouncing:** worsens relations and marks a rival. AI governments usually denounce a country before they attack it.
- **Pacts:** 48-hour non-aggression pacts.
- **Alliances:** up to 6 nations that defend each other.

**The AI.** Each AI government has a skill and a personality, and plays by the same rules and costs as you.

- **Skill:** from Novice to Mastermind. A Mastermind judges strength well, moves troops to threatened borders, builds forts, mixes unit types and counts your allies before it attacks. A Novice misjudges and keeps its army at home.
- **Personality:** aggressive, expansionist, balanced, defensive, diplomatic or mercantile.

**World domination.** A nation that holds 50% of the world's value (economy, people and land) wins the era. If nobody does, the strongest nation wins when the era ends after 90 days. The winner enters the Hall of Fame. Then the map resets and every leader starts again from their homeland.
