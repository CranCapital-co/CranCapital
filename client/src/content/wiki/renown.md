# Renown: levels, ranks and rewards

Renown is your level. It goes up with almost everything you do, and every level brings something.

**Where XP comes from**

- Sales (a little every few minutes while your businesses sell), trades on the exchange, building and upgrading, contracts, city projects and travel.
- Your nation: recruiting, finishing buildings and research, winning battles, taking territory, alliances and peace treaties.
- Missions, achievements, the daily reward and crates.

**What you get**

- **Every level:** a perk point and a supply crate.
- **Every 5 levels:** a new rank (Hustler, Trader, Merchant, Magnate, Tycoon, Mogul, Baron, Titan, Sovereign, Emperor, then Legend), a gold crate and the rank as a title.
- **New things to do:**

  | Level | Unlocks |
  | --- | --- |
  | 3 | Research |
  | 4 | Artillery and forts |
  | 5 | Armour and declaring war |
  | 6 | Alliances and pacts |
  | 8 | Labs and air defence |
  | 10 | Air wings |
  | 12 | Fleets |
  | 15 | Master perks |
  | 20 | Missiles |

Early levels take minutes. Later ones take longer, but there is always a next level.

**Perks.** Spend perk points in five branches. Rank 1 costs 1 point, rank 2 costs 2, and so on, up to rank 5. Ranks 4 and 5 open at level 15.

- **Trade:** +2% on your sales a rank, paid to you as a bonus.
- **Fortune:** +5% XP a rank, and gold crates come sooner.
- **Command:** +4% army strength and 4% cheaper recruits a rank.
- **Statecraft:** +5% national income and research a rank.
- **Logistics:** +6% army speed and 5% less upkeep a rank.

You can reset your perks for free once a week. After that, a reset costs a respec token, which you can find in gold crates.

**Crates.** Open them from the Renown window. A crate holds cash, XP, loyalty points, an XP boost, research, troops or money for your treasury. Every 10th supply crate is guaranteed to be gold. Crates are never sold for real money.

**Daily reward.** Collect once a day. The reward climbs over a 7-day cycle, with a crate on day 3 and a gold crate on day 7. Missing a day never resets the cycle. It only restarts your streak, which adds a little XP each day. Each 7-day streak earns a shield (up to 2), and a shield covers a missed day.

**Missions.** Three daily missions and, from level 2, three weekly ones. Each pays XP, and weekly missions also give a crate.

**Achievements.** Bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals for your business and your nation. They pay XP, and gold and platinum also give crates.

**Season.** A free 40-tier track each month. Every 400 XP you earn that month is a tier, and every tier has a reward. Tiers 20 and 40 give a season title.
