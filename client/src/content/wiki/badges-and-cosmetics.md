# Badges and cosmetics

Your profile shows who you are in the world. None of it can be bought with real money.

## Your identity

- Display name: what other players see.
- Title: a short line under your name.
- Avatar skin: how your avatar looks on the map.
- Player number: your place in the join order. Player 1 joined first.
- Net-worth ring: a ring around your avatar that shows how wealthy you are.

## The net-worth ring

The ring changes as your net worth grows:

| Ring | Net worth |
| --- | --- |
| Plain | under $50,000 |
| Bronze | $50,000 |
| Silver | $500,000 |
| Gold | $5,000,000 |
| Diamond | $50,000,000 |

Everyone can see it on the map.

## Badges

Badges mark milestones. Each is earned once and shown on your profile.

| Badge | How to earn it |
| --- | --- |
| Graduate | Finish Tutorial Island |
| First sale | Sell to your first customer |
| Trader | Trade on the exchange |
| Six figures | Reach $100,000 in net worth |
| Millionaire | Reach $1,000,000 in net worth |
| Open for business | Open a shop or restaurant in a rented room |
| Landowner | Buy your first plot of land |
| Landlord | Buy your first building |
| Prospector | Pay for your first mineral survey |
| Strike! | Open your first mine |
| Industrialist | Build your first factory |
| Exporter | Sell your first export through a port |
| Globetrotter | Visit every launch city |
| Banker | Open a bank |
| Public company | Take your company public |
| Bug hunter | Report a bug that staff confirm |
| Founding player | Join in the first month |

## Loyalty points

You earn 10 loyalty points for each day you play. One visit a day is enough.

Loyalty points buy cosmetics only. They never buy money, goods or any advantage.

## Referral race

Invite friends with your referral link. Each month the top inviters win a trophy.

| Place | Trophy |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | Diamond inviter |
| 2nd | Gold inviter |
| 3rd | Silver inviter |
| 4th | Bronze inviter |

Trophies are cosmetic. Unlike badges, you can win them again in later months.

Inviting fake accounts breaks the rules. One account per person.

## Leaderboards

Three leaderboards rank players:

- Richest, by net worth.
- Landowners, by property.
- Exporters, by export income.

Each has a board per city and a worldwide board. A fresh monthly board starts each month.

## Nothing here is for sale

Cosmetics, badges and trophies have no real-world value. They can't be bought, sold or traded for real money.
