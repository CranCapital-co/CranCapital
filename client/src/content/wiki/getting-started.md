# Getting started

CranCapital is a shared business game played on a real map. You start small with a food cart and grow from there.

Everyone plays in the same world. The streets and buildings are real places in London, New York, Tokyo, Singapore, Paris, Berlin, Toronto, Sydney, Shanghai, Moscow, Rio de Janeiro, Mexico City, Mumbai and Lagos. You start in the city of the country you lead, or the nearest one if your country has no city yet.

## Your first ten minutes

1. Pick a city. Your first arrival is free.
2. Walk your avatar to a busy street. Stations, sights and shopping streets have the most foot traffic.
3. Buy a food cart for $5,000 and park it. You start with $10,000, so half is left for stock.
4. Check the menu. A new cart sells Garden Salad, Hot Chips and Onigiri at their fair prices.
5. Watch the first sales come in. Your first cart comes with free starter stock.
6. Open the exchange and buy more ingredients before storage runs low.

## Starter stock

Your very first cart comes with these ingredients, free:

| Tomato | 400 |
| Potato | 600 |

## What to aim for

Your net worth unlocks new kinds of business. The first goal is $20,000, which opens shops and restaurants.

| Tier | Unlocks | Net worth needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Food carts | $0 |
| 2 | Shops and restaurants | $20,000 |
| 3 | Land and buildings | $150,000 |
| 4 | Mining and surveys | $750,000 |
| 5 | Industry: factories and ports | $3,000,000 |
| 6 | Finance | $15,000,000 |

## Good to know

- In-game money has no real-world value. You can't buy it and you can't cash it out.
- Your businesses keep selling for up to 24 hours after you were last online.
- The game never asks for your real location. Your position is only where your avatar stands.
- You can run up to 5 carts at once.
