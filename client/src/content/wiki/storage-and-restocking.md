# Storage and restocking

Your businesses use ingredients from your storage. If storage runs dry, sales stop.

## How storage works

- You have one storage per city. All your businesses in that city share it.
- Each city gives you 200,000 units of storage for free.
- A storage yard adds 250,000 units per level. Yards unlock at tier 3.
- Stock in London can't feed a cart in Tokyo. Ports can ship goods between cities at tier 5.

## Restocking

Buy ingredients on [the exchange](#/wiki/the-exchange). Choose which city's storage they go to.

A dish sells only when you hold every ingredient it needs. When one runs out, that dish is marked out of stock. Your other dishes keep selling.

## How much to buy

Work out a day's needs from your sales. For example, a cart selling 300 Garden Salads a day uses 900 lettuce and 600 tomato.

Remember that your businesses run for up to 24 hours while you are away. Stock enough to cover that.

## When storage is full

Farms, mines and factories put what they make into your storage. If it is full, the extra is lost.

To avoid that, you can:

- Build or upgrade a storage yard.
- Sell stock on the exchange.
- Switch a producer to auto-sell. It then sells its output straight to market traders.

Shipments from your ports wait at the dock until there is room.

## Stock counts towards net worth

Goods in storage count towards your net worth. They are valued at the price market traders would pay for them.
