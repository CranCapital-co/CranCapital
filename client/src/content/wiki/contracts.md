# Contracts

Hotels, caterers, builders and stores in each city post orders: "A hotel near Trafalgar Square wants 400 lettuce by 6 pm." Find them on the Empire window, **City jobs** tab.

## How they work

1. **Take it.** You pay a deposit (10% of the goods' market value). Contracts open at tier 2, and you can hold three at a time.
2. **Get the goods** into your storage in that city: grow or make them, or buy them on the exchange.
3. **Deliver** before the deadline. You're paid about 30% above the market price, and your deposit comes back.

Miss the deadline and the contract fails: the deposit is lost.

New orders come in every hour, and the deadlines are three to eight hours away.
