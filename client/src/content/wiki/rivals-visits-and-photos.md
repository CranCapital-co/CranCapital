# Rivals, visits and photos

## Street rivals

When another player's cart nearby sells the same dish as yours, both carts get a small flag on the map (yours is gold), and your cart's Business window shows a friendly price comparison: who sells what, for how much, and whether you're cheaper.

## Visit an empire

Tap a player (on the map or a leaderboard) and choose **Visit their empire**. The camera tours their businesses in your city one by one. Tap the map or **Stop** to end the tour.

## Photo mode

Open **More**, then **Photo mode**. The interface hides so you can frame the city:

- Drag, pinch and tilt with two fingers to move the camera.
- Slide the time of day to see the city at dawn, golden hour or night.
- Pick a filter: Natural, Warm, Vivid, Film or Mono.
- **Save picture**: on iPad the share sheet opens, so you can save it to Photos.
