# Land, farms and buildings

Tier 3 unlocks at $150,000 net worth. You can now own property instead of renting it.

## Plots

The map is divided into plots of 25 by 25 metres. You can buy any free plot that can be built on.

Walk to within 60 metres of the plot to buy it. The price starts from $30,000 and changes with the city and foot traffic.

| City | Typical plot price, quiet to busy |
| --- | --- |
| Singapore | about $21,600 to $36,000 |
| Tokyo | about $23,400 to $39,000 |
| London | about $25,200 to $42,000 |
| New York | about $27,000 to $45,000 |

Quiet land is cheaper, and farms don't need foot traffic. Save the busy plots for apartments.

## What you can build on a plot

| Building | Cost | Running cost per hour | Crew | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Storage yard | $25,000 | $3 | 0 | Adds 250,000 units of storage in that city per level |
| Tree farm | $35,000 | $6 | 1 | Grows 36 timber an hour |
| Farm | $40,000 | $8 | 1 | Grows one crop of your choice |
| Apartment block | $80,000 | $5 | 1 | 40 homes per level, rented to local tenants |

Each plot holds one building. Crews are hired for you and paid around the clock at the city's wage.

If you can't pay the crew or the running costs, the building closes. Add money and reopen it.

## Farms

A farm grows one crop and puts it straight into your storage. You can change the crop later.

| Crop | Units per hour at level 1 | Base price | Value per hour at base prices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wheat | 3,600 | $0.05 | $180 |
| Wasabi | 3,000 | $0.06 | $180 |
| Potato | 2,600 | $0.07 | $182 |
| Lettuce | 2,250 | $0.08 | $180 |
| Rice | 2,000 | $0.09 | $180 |
| Tomato | 1,500 | $0.12 | $180 |
| Banana | 1,200 | $0.15 | $180 |
| Pumpkins | 550 | $0.33 | $181.50 |
| Coffee beans | 450 | $0.40 | $180 |

Bananas and coffee beans grow only in warm cities. Singapore is the only warm city at launch.

Every crop earns about the same at base prices. Grow what your own shops use, or whatever is trading above its base price.

A farm fills storage quickly. A level 1 wheat farm makes 86,400 units a day. Watch your storage space or turn on auto-sell.

## Upgrading producers

Farms and tree farms go up to level 5. Output grows with each level.

| Level | Output compared with level 1 |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1.0 times |
| 2 | 1.8 times |
| 3 | 2.6 times |
| 4 | 3.4 times |
| 5 | 4.2 times |

The upgrade from level 1 costs 1.5 times the building's price. Later upgrades cost 3, 4.5 and 6 times the price.

Running costs and crew size grow by half the base amount per level.

## Apartment blocks

An apartment block has 40 homes per level. Local tenants pay rent every day, a little at a time.

Rent starts from $70 a day per home. It is higher on busy plots and in more expensive cities.

Before those two adjustments, a level 1 block brings in about $2,380 a day.

## Buying whole buildings

You can buy almost any real building on the map. Landmarks belong to the city and can't be bought.

The city sells a building for 90 days of its full rent. Bigger and busier buildings cost more.

As the owner you can:

- Earn rent from players who rent rooms there.
- Earn rent from local tenants in the rooms no player has taken.
- Set rent between 50% and 200% of the going rate.
- Rename the building.
- Run your own shops there without paying rent.
- List the building for sale to other players at your own price.

High rent scares off local tenants and may send players elsewhere. Low rent fills rooms.

When a tenant goes 3 days without paying, their lease ends and the room is free again.

## Property tax

You pay property tax on plots and buildings you own. It is 0.05% of the purchase price per day.

On a $40,000 plot that is $20 a day.

## Selling property

- Sell a plot or building back to the city for 70% of what you paid.
- Or list it for sale to other players at any price you like.
- A plot listed for sale is sold with whatever stands on it.
- To sell a plot to the city, first sell what is built on it.
- Buildings on plots sell back for 50% of what you spent on them.

## Pausing while you are away

Rent, tax, crops and wages all pause once you have been offline for 24 hours. See [Offline earnings](#/wiki/offline-earnings).
