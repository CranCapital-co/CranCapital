# Ports and exports

Ports unlock at tier 5, with $3,000,000 net worth. A port moves your goods between cities and sells them overseas.

## Building a port

A port costs $750,000. It must stand on a plot you own right beside open water.

It costs $30 an hour to run and needs a crew of 10.

## Two kinds of shipment

From a port you can send goods from that city's storage to any other city.

- A transfer moves goods into your own storage in the other city.
- An export sells the goods to overseas buyers in the other city.

Transfers are how a farm in Singapore can feed your cafés in London.

## Time and cost

Shipping takes 8 minutes, plus about 1 minute for every 600 km.

Freight costs 2% of the goods' base value, plus a little more for distance. A 10,000 km trip costs about 4.5% in total.

You pay freight when the ship leaves.

A port can load 50,000 units an hour per level. Upgrade it to ship more.

If your storage in the other city is full, a transfer waits at the dock until there is room.

## Export prices

Overseas buyers pay more than the base price. In most cases they pay 1.2 times base.

Some cities pay extra for certain goods:

| City | Goods | Buyers pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| London | Furniture | 1.7 times base |
| London | Coffee beans, bananas | 1.65 times base |
| London | Materials such as steel, glass and concrete | 1.3 times base |
| New York | Coffee beans, bananas | 1.65 times base |
| New York | Electronics, furniture | 1.35 times base |
| Tokyo | Coffee beans, bananas | 1.65 times base |
| Tokyo | Raw materials and diesel | 1.4 times base |
| Tokyo | Wasabi | 0.95 times base |
| Singapore | Farm goods | 1.45 times base |
| Singapore | Materials such as steel, glass and concrete | 1.3 times base |
| Anywhere else | Everything not listed | 1.2 times base |

You see an estimated price when you send the shipment. You are paid when it arrives.

## Big loads lower the price

Overseas buyers only want so much at once. A large shipment pushes their price down as it sells.

The price then recovers slowly over time. Several smaller shipments, spread out, usually earn more than one huge one.

Other players' exports of the same good to the same city also lower the price.

## Is exporting worth it?

Compare the export price with the exchange. Market traders buy at about 10% below the reference price.

An export at 1.2 times base, minus freight, usually beats that. The special goods in the table do much better.

## Leaderboards

Export income counts towards the exporters leaderboard. Your first export also earns the Exporter badge.
