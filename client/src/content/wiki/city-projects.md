# City projects

Every city has a big project that all its players build together, starting with a market hall near the start point. You can watch it rise on the map.

- Open the Empire window, **City jobs** tab, to see what the project still needs (concrete, steel, glass, planks).
- Tap **Give** to hand over goods from your storage in that city. You only give what it still needs.
- The city thanks you with 60% of the goods' market value in cash, plus loyalty points.
- The top givers are listed on the project.
- When everything has arrived, the market hall opens with stalls and flags, and foot traffic within 400 m rises by 15% for good. Everyone who gave gets a thank-you.
