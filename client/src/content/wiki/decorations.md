# Decorations

Make your businesses look like yours: flower boxes, potted trees, a gold striped awning, bunting, string lights, a neon sign or a red carpet.

- Open a business, then the **Decorate** tab.
- Decorations cost loyalty points (from daily goals, city projects and days played), never cash.
- They're looks only: they never change sales, customers or prices.
- Everyone sees them on the map. Lights and neon glow at night.
- **Take down** removes one (the points aren't refunded).
