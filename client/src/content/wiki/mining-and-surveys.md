# Mining and surveys

Tier 4 unlocks at $750,000 net worth. Raw materials lie under the cities, and you have to find them first.

## Surveys

A survey costs $20,000. Pick **Survey** in the map tools, then tap the area you want: it covers that 200 metre block.

The results appear on your map, and only on yours. Other players can't see what you found.

Deposits are the same for everyone, though. Another player who surveys the same block will find the same deposit.

Roughly what to expect from a block:

| Result | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | about 30% |
| One deposit | about 48% |
| Two deposits | about 22% |

You can't survey the same block twice. Blocks with no buildable land hold nothing.

## What you might find

| Deposit | How common | Size in units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sand | 20% of finds | 150,000 to 600,000 |
| Limestone | 20% of finds | 90,000 to 350,000 |
| Coal | 20% of finds | 40,000 to 160,000 |
| Iron ore | 18% of finds | 35,000 to 140,000 |
| Crude oil | 12% of finds | 35,000 to 150,000 |
| Copper ore | 10% of finds | 25,000 to 110,000 |

## Building a mine

Each deposit sits under one plot. To mine it you need three things:

1. You surveyed the block yourself.
2. You own the plot above the deposit.
3. Nobody else is already mining it.

Only one mine can work a deposit. If someone else owns the plot, you would have to buy it from them.

| Mine | Cost | Running cost per hour | Crew | Output per hour at level 1 | Base price of output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sand pit | $100,000 | $6 | 2 | 1,100 sand | $1.50 |
| Limestone quarry | $120,000 | $8 | 3 | 540 limestone | $3.00 |
| Coal mine | $150,000 | $10 | 3 | 240 coal | $7.00 |
| Iron mine | $150,000 | $10 | 3 | 200 iron ore | $8.00 |
| Copper mine | $160,000 | $10 | 3 | 180 copper ore | $9.00 |
| Oil well | $180,000 | $12 | 3 | 200 crude oil | $8.00 |

Mines run day and night. Crews are paid around the clock at the city's wage.

## Diesel

Mines burn diesel from your storage in the same city. Higher levels burn more.

With no diesel, the mine stops digging but you still pay the crew. Keep a reserve.

Diesel has a base price of $15 a unit. You can buy it on the exchange, or refine it yourself at tier 5.

## Deposits run out

Every unit you dig comes off the deposit. When it reaches zero the mine is worked out.

Sell the empty mine back to the city and survey for a new deposit. Bigger deposits are worth more, so check the size before you build.

## Upgrades

Mines go up to level 5. Output follows the same steps as other producers: 1.8, 2.6, 3.4 and 4.2 times.

A faster mine also empties its deposit faster.

## Why private information matters

A survey result is something only you know. You can use it in several ways:

- Buy the plot quietly and mine it.
- Buy the plot and hold it for later.
- Sell the plot to another player for more than you paid.
