# Daily goals

Every day you get three small goals, shown on the Empire window's Overview. Finish one and tap **Claim** to collect loyalty points.

- New players start with three easy ones: sell 10 meals, earn $500 and change a price on your menu.
- After that the goals mix it up: sell things, earn from sales, trade on the exchange, upgrade a business, deliver a contract or give to a city project. Targets grow with your tier.
- Progress counts from when the goal appeared, using real sales and trades.
- Goals pay loyalty points, not cash. Spend points on shop decorations.
- New goals arrive at midnight (UTC). Unclaimed goals don't carry over, so claim them the same day.
