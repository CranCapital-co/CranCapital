# Shops and restaurants

Tier 2 unlocks at $20,000 net worth. You can now rent a room in a real building and open a shop.

## Business types

| Business | Licence cost | Running cost per hour | Customers one staff member can serve per hour | Default hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Café | $8,000 | $1.00 | 18 | 7:00 to 18:00 |
| Grocery | $10,000 | $1.50 | 25 | 9:00 to 20:00 |
| Restaurant | $15,000 | $2.00 | 10 | 11:00 to 23:00 |
| Furniture store | $25,000 | $3.00 | 6 | 9:00 to 20:00 |
| Electronics store | $30,000 | $3.00 | 6 | 9:00 to 20:00 |

Furniture and electronics stores see few customers, but each sale is large.

## Renting a room

Tap a building with a free room and rent it. You pay the licence and the first day's rent up front.

After that, rent is taken a little at a time through the day.

Daily rent starts from a base of $50. It then changes with three things:

- Foot traffic. Busy buildings cost more.
- The kind of building. Stations cost about 60% more and landmarks about 50% more than offices.
- The city. See [Travel and cities](#/wiki/travel-and-cities).

If a player owns the building, they may set rent between 50% and 200% of the going rate. You pay no rent in a building you own.

## Unpaid rent

If you can't pay, the rent builds up as a debt. Paying it off clears the warning.

After 3 days of unpaid rent the lease ends. The business is then abandoned and you lose it.

## Staff

Rooms need staff. Each item that adds capacity makes room for 2 staff, up to 30 in total.

Your room serves the smaller of two numbers: what the staff can handle, and what the fit-out allows. Hiring beyond your fit-out wastes wages.

Staff are paid by the hour, only while the business is open.

| City | Wage per staff member per hour |
| --- | --- |
| Singapore | $13 |
| Tokyo | $14 |
| London | $16 |
| New York | $18 |

If you can't pay wages, your staff quit. Hire again once you have money.

## Opening hours

You choose when the room opens and closes, in the city's local time. Wages run only during open hours, so match the hours to your busy times.

Rent and running costs are charged all day.

## Upgrades

Rooms go up to level 5. Each level adds a menu slot, 10% more capacity and a little attractiveness.

The upgrade from level 1 costs 1.5 times the licence. Later upgrades cost 3, 4.5 and 6 times the licence.

## Closing down

You can sell a business back to the city for 50% of what you spent on it. The lease ends when you do.
