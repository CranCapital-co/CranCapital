# Industry and recipes

Tier 5 unlocks at $3,000,000 net worth. Factories turn inputs into more valuable goods.

## How a factory works

Each factory runs one recipe. Every cycle it takes the inputs from your storage and adds the outputs.

Inputs, outputs and diesel all come from your storage in the factory's city. Factories are built on plots you own.

A factory runs day and night. Its crew is paid around the clock at the city's wage.

## Supply chains

Finished goods need several steps. Here are the two longest chains.

Furniture:

1. A tree farm grows timber.
2. A sawmill cuts timber into planks.
3. Mines dig iron ore and coal.
4. A steel mill makes steel.
5. A furniture factory builds furniture from 4 planks and 1 steel.
6. A furniture store sells it, or you sell it on the exchange.

Electronics:

1. Mines dig copper ore, coal and sand, and a well pumps crude oil.
2. A smelter makes copper.
3. A refinery makes plastic, with diesel as a second product.
4. A glassworks makes glass.
5. An electronics plant assembles one unit from one of each.
6. An electronics store sells it, or you sell it on the exchange.

You don't have to own every step. Buy any input on the exchange and focus on the steps you run well.

## When a factory stops

A factory shows a warning when something is missing:

- No inputs: one of the ingredients ran out.
- No fuel: there is not enough diesel.
- Storage full: there is no room for the output.

It starts again by itself once the problem is fixed.

## Auto-sell

Turn on auto-sell and the factory sells its output straight to market traders. Nothing goes into storage.

This is handy when storage is tight. You get the traders' buying price, which is about 10% below the reference price.

## Upgrades

Factories go up to level 5. Speed follows the usual steps: 1.8, 2.6, 3.4 and 4.2 times.

Diesel use, running costs and crew size also grow with level.

To move goods between cities, see [Ports and exports](#/wiki/ports-and-exports).
