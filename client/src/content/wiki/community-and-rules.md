# Community and rules

CranCapital is a shared world. This page covers how to talk to other players and what is expected.

The full rules are on the [community rules](#/legal/rules) page.

## Ways to talk

- City chat: one open channel for each city.
- Channels: rooms that players create themselves.
- Direct messages: private talk between two players.
- Friends: add players to find them again easily.

Chat allows up to 12 messages a minute per player.

## Boards

Two public boards help shape the game:

- Bug Reports: tell us what is broken.
- Suggestions: propose new ideas.

Everyone can vote on posts. A bug that staff confirm earns you the Bug hunter badge.

## Discord directory

Players can list their Discord servers in a directory. This is the only place in the game with links.

The game builds each link itself from the server's invite code.

## The rules in short

- No links anywhere, except through the Discord directory.
- No harassment, slurs or hate.
- No spam.
- No pretending to be staff or another player.
- No sharing other people's personal details.
- No cheating, bots or modified clients.
- No exploiting bugs. Report them instead.
- No off-site services that take in-game money.

Ordinary swearing is fine. Attacking people is not.

## Filters

Slurs are masked as you type, and you see a notice. A message or name with a link is refused, with the reason shown.

Nothing is hidden without telling you. There is no shadow-banning.

## Mute, block and report

- Block a player (from their profile or a message’s menu) to hide their chat messages from you and stop direct messages both ways. They aren’t told.
- Report a player or a message from its menu.

Reports go to the moderation team, who are real people.

## How moderation works

Staff can warn a player, remove content, suspend an account or ban it.

A person makes every one of those decisions. The player is always told the reason.

You can appeal a decision. The [terms](#/legal/terms) explain how.

## Reporting illegal content

Anyone can report illegal content with the public form at [#/report](#/report). You don't need an account.

## Money stays in the game

In-game money has no real-world value. It can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

Trading game money or items for real money is banned.
