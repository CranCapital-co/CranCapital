# Travel and cities

CranCapital has 14 cities: London, New York, Tokyo, Singapore, Paris, Berlin, Toronto, Sydney, Shanghai, Moscow, Rio de Janeiro, Mexico City, Mumbai and Lagos. Each uses real streets and buildings. You start in the city of the country you lead (or the nearest one), and can switch to any other at any time.

## The four cities

| City | Wage per hour | Price level | Climate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore | $13 | 1.2 | Warm |
| Tokyo | $14 | 1.3 | Temperate |
| London | $16 | 1.4 | Temperate |
| New York | $18 | 1.5 | Temperate |

The price level raises rents, plot prices and apartment income in that city. Fair prices for dishes are the same everywhere.

So an expensive city costs more to work in, but its property also earns more.

Bananas and coffee beans grow only in a warm climate: Singapore, Rio de Janeiro, Mumbai and Lagos.

## Local time

Each city runs on its real local time. Lunch in Tokyo happens while London sleeps.

Owning businesses in several cities spreads your busy hours around the clock.

## Looking around

There's no character to walk around. You look down on the city and can tap anything in it: a street to park a cart, a building to rent or buy, a plot of land, a block to survey. Drag and pinch to move and zoom.

The game never asks for your real location.

## Switching cities

Open **More**, then **Cities**, and tap **Go to** a city. You're there at once, and it's free.

Your businesses everywhere keep running while you look at another city.

## Storage is per city

Each city has its own storage. Goods bought for London stay in London.

To move goods between cities you need a port. See [Ports and exports](#/wiki/ports-and-exports).

Visit every launch city to earn the Globetrotter badge.
