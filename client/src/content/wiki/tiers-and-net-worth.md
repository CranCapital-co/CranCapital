# Tiers and net worth

The game has six tiers. Each one unlocks when your net worth reaches a set amount.

## The six tiers

| Tier | Name | Net worth needed | The new idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Food carts | $0 | Location and pricing |
| 2 | Shops and restaurants | $20,000 | Rent, staff wages, fit-out, selling while offline |
| 3 | Land and buildings | $150,000 | Owning property and earning as a landlord |
| 4 | Mining and surveys | $750,000 | Raw materials and private information |
| 5 | Industry: factories and ports | $3,000,000 | Supply chains, shipping time and cost |
| 6 | Finance | $15,000,000 | Credit, risk and investment |

Each tier adds one new idea on top of the last. Nothing from an earlier tier stops being useful.

## What counts as net worth

Net worth is everything you own, added up:

- Your cash.
- Your businesses, at what you spent on them, including upgrades and fit-out.
- Your plots and buildings, at the price you paid.
- Goods in storage, at the price market traders would pay.
- Money and goods tied up in open exchange orders.
- Goods on ships.
- Your finance holdings at tier 6.

Net worth is refreshed every few minutes and after big purchases.

## Spending doesn't lower it much

Buying a business turns cash into an asset. Your net worth stays about the same.

Wages, rent, running costs and tax do lower it. So does selling something back to the city.

## Upgrade costs

Carts go up to level 3. Everything else goes up to level 5.

| Upgrade | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 to 2 | 1.5 times the purchase price |
| Level 2 to 3 | 3 times the purchase price |
| Level 3 to 4 | 4.5 times the purchase price |
| Level 4 to 5 | 6 times the purchase price |

## Your net-worth ring

A ring around your picture shows your net worth to others. It moves from bronze to silver, gold and diamond.

## Leaderboards

Three boards rank players: richest, landowners and exporters. Each exists per city and worldwide.

A fresh monthly board starts every month, so new players can compete too.
