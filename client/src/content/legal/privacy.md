# Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains what CranCapital knows about you, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

If anything is unclear, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

## The short version

- We collect only what the game needs to run and stay safe.
- We never ask for or store your real-world location.
- We don't sell your data. There are no ads.
- There is no analytics unless you turn it on.
- You can download your data or delete your account at any time.

## Who is responsible

CranCapital is responsible for your personal data. In legal terms, we are the "controller".

Contact us about anything on this page at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

### Account details

- Your email address. You sign in with an email link or code, or with Google.
- If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic account identifier from Google.
- Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth. The game stores no passwords.
- Your confirmation that you are 13 or older.

### Profile

- Your display name, title and avatar choices.
- Your player number, badges and loyalty points.

### Game data and play history

- Your businesses, property, storage, orders and trades.
- A history of your in-game money, such as sales, purchases and rent.
- Your avatar's position on the game map and the cities you visit.
- When you were last online, and how many days you have played.

### Things you write

- Chat messages, channel posts and direct messages.
- Posts and votes on the Bug Reports and Suggestions boards.
- Discord servers you list in the directory.

Direct messages are private between you and the other player. Staff read them only when someone reports them, or when needed to look into a safety problem.

### Reports

- Reports you send about players, messages or illegal content.
- Any contact details you choose to add to the public report form.

### Technical logs

- Basic logs such as your IP address, browser type and request times.
- Supabase holds these logs for security and to keep the service working.

## What we do not collect

- Your real-world location. The game never asks for it. Your avatar's position is a game position only.
- Payment details. Nothing in the game is sold.
- Advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no ad trackers.
- Analytics data, unless you switch analytics on in settings. You can switch it off again at any time.

## Why we use your data

We use your data for three purposes:

1. Running the game. This covers signing you in, saving progress and showing your businesses to others.
2. Safety and moderation. This covers handling reports, enforcing the rules and dealing with illegal content.
3. Preventing cheating. This covers spotting bots, modified clients and multiple accounts.

## Our legal bases

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR we need a legal reason for each use. In plain words:

| What we do | Legal basis | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create your account and run the game for you | Contract | We need this data to provide the game you signed up for. |
| Moderation, security logs and anti-cheat | Legitimate interests | We and other players have a fair interest in a safe, honest game. |
| Handling reports of illegal content | Legitimate interests, and legal obligation where the law requires action | We must be able to act on reports. |
| Optional notifications | Consent | We only send them if you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time. |
| Optional analytics | Consent | Off unless you turn it on. You can turn it off at any time. |

You can object to uses based on legitimate interests. See "Your rights" below.

## What other players can see

A shared world means some things are public inside the game:

- Your display name, title, avatar, player number, badges and net-worth ring.
- Your businesses and property on the map, with your name on them.
- Your place on leaderboards.
- Messages you post in city chat, channels and boards.

Other players never see your email address or your IP address.

## Where your data is stored

| Service | What it does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Stores accounts, game data, messages and logs | European Union, region eu-west-3 (Paris, France) |
| GitHub Pages | Serves the game's static files, such as code and images | GitHub's servers; GitHub sees your IP address when it serves the files |
| OpenFreeMap | Serves the map tiles | OpenFreeMap's servers; OpenFreeMap sees your IP address when it serves tiles |
| Google | Only if you choose to sign in with Google | Google's servers |

These services act on our instructions or provide a standard public service. Each has its own privacy policy.

GitHub and Google may process data outside the UK and EU. Where that happens, they rely on recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Account, profile and game data | Until you delete your account |
| Data after a deletion request | Removed within 30 days of the request |
| Moderation records, such as warnings and bans | Up to 12 months |
| Backups | Roll off within 30 days |

When you delete your account, your personal data is removed. Your businesses stay on the map as abandoned buildings, with no link to you.

## Your rights

You have these rights over your personal data:

- Access. Use "Download my data" in settings to get a copy.
- Deletion. Use the Danger zone in settings to delete your account.
- Correction. Change your display name and profile in settings, or ask us to fix something.
- Objection. Ask us to stop a use that relies on legitimate interests.
- Restriction and portability. Ask us to pause a use, or to give you your data in a common format.
- Withdrawing consent. Turn off notifications or analytics at any time.

For anything the settings don't cover, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EU it is your local data protection authority.

## Children

You must be at least 13 to play. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will remove it.

In some countries the age for consenting to data use is higher than 13. If that applies to you, ask a parent or guardian before turning on optional features.

## Cookies and local storage

The game keeps a small amount of data in your browser. It holds your sign-in session and your settings.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

## No selling, no ads

We don't sell or rent your personal data. We don't show ads. We don't build advertising profiles.

## Automated decisions

Filters act automatically on chat, such as masking slurs and refusing links. They tell you when they do.

Warnings, suspensions and bans are always decided by a person.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the game.
