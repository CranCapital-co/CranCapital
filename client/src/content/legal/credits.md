# Credits

CranCapital is built on the work of many people. Thank you to all of them.

## Game

CranCapital is based on Capital Rift by NIKS GAMES LLC, used with their permission.

## Map data

© OpenStreetMap contributors. See [openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The map data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

The streets and buildings in the game are real places, mapped by OpenStreetMap volunteers around the world.

## Map tiles

Map tiles are served by [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org).

The tiles are built with the OpenMapTiles schema. © [OpenMapTiles](https://openmaptiles.org).

## Weather

Live weather for each city comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), free for non-commercial use. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## World map

Country shapes, populations and economies come from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (1:110m countries), which is in the public domain. Country names and borders in the game follow Natural Earth and are not a statement about any real dispute.

## Sun position

The sun's position is worked out with SunCalc by Vladimir Agafonkin (BSD-2-Clause).

## City data made for the game

The game uses its own city data: a building list, a foot-traffic grid and a plot grid for each city.

This data is derived from OpenStreetMap. It is available under the ODbL in the game's public repository.
