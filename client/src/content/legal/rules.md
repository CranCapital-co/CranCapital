# Community rules

CranCapital is one shared world. These rules keep it friendly and fair for everyone.

They apply everywhere you can write or choose a name. That includes chat, channels, direct messages, boards, display names and business names.

## Be decent to each other

- No harassment. Don't target, threaten, stalk or keep bothering someone.
- No slurs.
- No hate. Don't attack people for who they are.
- No spam. Don't flood chat or repeat the same message or advert.
- No impersonation. Don't pretend to be staff or another player.
- No sharing other people's personal details. That includes real names, addresses, phone numbers, photos and accounts elsewhere.
- Nothing illegal, and nothing sexual involving minors, ever.

Ordinary swearing is allowed. Swearing at someone to hurt them is harassment.

Competing hard is part of the game. Undercutting a rival's prices is fine. Abusing them in chat is not.

## No links

Links aren't allowed anywhere in the game. This keeps scams and harmful sites out.

The one exception is the Discord directory. You enter your server's invite there, and the game builds the link itself.

Don't try to get around this by spelling out or disguising an address.

## Play fair

- No cheating.
- No bots, scripts or automation that plays for you.
- No modified clients.
- No exploiting bugs. Report them on the Bug Reports board instead.
- One account per person.

Reporting a bug is always welcome. A bug that staff confirm earns you the Bug hunter badge.

## Keep money in the game

In-game money has no real-world value. It can't be bought or cashed out.

- Don't sell or buy in-game money, items or accounts for real money.
- Don't run or advertise off-site services that take in-game money. That includes gambling, lotteries and paid "boosting".

Lending and trading inside the game, using the game's own tools, is fine.

## How the filters work

Two filters run automatically. Both tell you what they did.

- Slurs are masked as you type, and you see a notice.
- A message or name that contains a link is refused. You are told why.

Filters never hide your message quietly. If a message was sent, others can see it.

## Mute and block

You are in control of what you see.

- Mute a player to stop seeing their messages in chat.
- Block a player to stop them sending you direct messages.

Neither one tells the other player. You can undo both at any time.

## Reporting

If someone breaks the rules, report it:

1. Open the menu on the message or the player's profile.
2. Choose Report.
3. Pick a reason and add a short note if it helps.

Reports go to the moderation team. Don't answer rule-breaking with more rule-breaking.

Deliberately false reports are themselves against the rules.

## Reporting illegal content without an account

Anyone can report illegal content with the public form at [#/report](#/report). You don't need an account to use it.

## What staff can do

Staff can take four kinds of action:

- Warn a player.
- Remove content, such as a message, post or name.
- Suspend an account for a set time.
- Ban an account.

Every action is taken by a person, never by a machine alone. The player is always given the reason.

Staff choose the action that fits. A slip usually earns a warning. Serious harm can mean a ban straight away.

## Nothing is hidden in secret

We don't shadow-ban. We don't silently hide messages or players.

If staff act on your account or your content, you will be told what happened and why.

## Appeals

If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal. The [terms of use](#/legal/terms) explain how.

## Staff follow the rules too

Staff will never ask for your sign-in code or email link. Anyone who does is not staff. Please report them.
